#### 2. RPG Progression & Gear Mechanics (`models/` & `services/game.py`)
- Core Stats:
  - Level & EXP: Gained via daily activities, working, heists, and winning duels.
  - Power (ATK): Determined by equipped weapons and gloves.
  - Defense (DEF): Determined by coat, body armor, and boots.
  - Drip / Charisma: Unlocked through high-tier cosmetics; increases luck and success in street heists and grants shop discounts.
  - Energy (Stamina): Consumed by actions, passively regenerates over time.
- Equipment System: Inventory slots for [Head, Body, Legs, Weapon, Accessory, Aura]. Equipping an item modifies base stats and updates the character's visual layer.

- Chat Activity Spawns (Raids): Maintain an in-memory/database message counter per group. After every 60-100 messages, spawn a random "Rogue Shadow" or urban boss with an inline button: `[⚔️ Strike Boss]`. Active chat members collaborate to defeat it and split the loot.
- Economy & Sinks:
  - Currencies: Credits (soft currency) and Soul Shards (rare currency).
  - Boutique & Black Market: Timed rotating shop with rarity tiers (Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary).
  - Risk/Reward Activities: Street heists with failure/arrest probabilities, and a mini casino (Dice, Coinflip) with a slight mathematical house edge.
